# Pearly Enterprise (পার্লি এন্টারপ্রাইজ)

Official website for **Pearly Enterprise**, an importer and wholesale supplier of spring steel wire based in Nawabpur Road, Dhaka — the central industrial hardware market of Bangladesh.

The business specializes in:
* **High-Carbon Spring Steel Wire** sourced directly from certified drawing mills in China (covering SWG 1 through SWG 34).
* **Stainless Steel Spring Wire** imported from partner manufacturers in India.
* Supplying both large container indents for industrial manufacturing plants and small batch orders for local spring makers and workshops.

---

## Website Overview

This website serves as the digital catalog and inquiry hub for industrial buyers, engineers, and spring manufacturers across Bangladesh.

* **Wire Gauge Reference (SWG 1 – 34)**: An interactive gauge table showing diameter conversions in millimeters (mm) and inches (in), with live search and 1-click WhatsApp quote requests for any specific gauge.
* **Dual Theme (Normal & Dark Blue)**: Features a crisp daylight corporate view and a deep midnight blue industrial theme designed for low glare.
* **Partner Mills & Warehouse Showcase**: Photo galleries presenting the wire drawing process at our partner mills in China, alongside ready stock at our Nawabpur showroom and warehouse.
* **Direct Communication**: Instant connections for pricing and orders through WhatsApp and WeChat, plus an inquiry form.
* **Legal Compliance**: Verifiable business credentials including Bangladesh Trade License, BIN, TIN, and tax filings.

## Updating Content & Photos

To add/remove gallery photos, wire gauge sizes, or update contact info, simply edit **`data/site-data.json`**:
* **Photos**: Add or remove entries under `"shopGallery"` or `"factoryGallery"`.
* **Contact Details**: Update numbers, address, or WeChat IDs under `"company"`.
* The website automatically loads from this JSON file.
